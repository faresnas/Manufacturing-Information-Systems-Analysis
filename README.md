
# 🏭 Manufacturing Information Systems Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

An academic Information Systems Engineering project at Braude College of Engineering, focused on analyzing business processes and information systems requirements at a plastics manufacturing company.

The project examined production operations, inventory management, employee management, and opportunities for business process improvement.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze existing business processes in a manufacturing environment.
- Gather and document organizational requirements.
- Identify operational challenges and inefficiencies.
- Model business processes using BPMN.
- Design a conceptual data model using ERD.
- Propose improvements through information systems and automation.

## 🔍 Analysis and Methodology

### Requirements Gathering
Conducted organizational research and a structured interview to understand business needs and existing workflows.

### Business Process Modeling (BPMN)
Analyzed current business processes and modeled proposed improvements.

### Data Modeling (ERD)
Designed an Entity-Relationship Diagram representing key business entities and their relationships.

### Decision Analysis
Used decision tables and a decision tree to represent organizational decision-making rules.

### Process Improvement
Examined opportunities to improve inventory monitoring, production coordination, employee management, and administrative workflows.
